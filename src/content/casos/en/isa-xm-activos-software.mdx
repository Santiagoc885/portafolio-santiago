## Context

Hybrid internship in software asset management (Software Asset Management), focused on Business Intelligence.

## Problem

License reports were mostly prepared by hand in Excel, gathering and consolidating the information periodically. Each update took about a day of repetitive work to consolidate and validate the data.

The team also needed to identify which licenses were unused or underused to support decisions about those assets.

## My responsibility

- Build dashboards in Power BI.
- Process and analyze asset and license data with Python.
- Automate the periodic reports.
- Identify unused or underused licenses.
- Support the analysis behind decisions on software assets.

## Analysis

The work covered more than 10,000 asset and license records, taken mainly from SharePoint, where licensing information was centralized.

To decide whether a license was underused, its level of usage and recorded activity was compared against the assigned license.

## Solution

An automated Python and Pandas flow that extracts, cleans and consolidates license data, plus Power BI dashboards to review the state of assets and licenses.

## Implementation

- Pandas for data manipulation and analysis.
- Periodic reports to make updating and tracking licenses easier.
- Totals and records checked after every update.

## Validation

Results were compared against the original data in SharePoint, and the indicators were reviewed with the responsible team. After every update, totals and records were checked to match.

## Learnings

I learned to work with real data end to end: extraction, cleaning, analysis and visualization in Power BI. I also learned that data quality has to be validated before presenting indicators.

Today I would design the process for automation, traceability and reuse of the data flow from the start.
