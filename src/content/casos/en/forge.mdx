## Context

Collaborative Riwi project (2026): a multi-tenant SaaS platform for onboarding and training operational teams.

## Problem

Onboarding and training new team members is hard when materials are scattered across manuals, documents and videos. FORGE lets each company centralize its training materials and offer AI assistance.

## My responsibility

I worked as a Full Stack Developer:

- Frontend and backend features of the platform.
- Integration of the NOVA AI assistant.
- Document management.
- Structure to support the multi-tenant system.

## Analysis

Scope was split into modules and features through user stories prioritized by sprint. The core platform features came first, followed by the AI, analytics and accessibility capabilities.

## Solution

- Content management: uploading documents, manuals and videos.
- **NOVA**: RAG-based assistant over the company's documents.
- Analytics.
- **ALAE**: accessibility component and inclusion assessment system.

## Implementation

Training documents are processed, split into fragments and turned into embeddings for semantic search. When a question comes in, the relevant fragments are retrieved and used as context for the model to generate the answer.

## Validation

Questions were asked about the uploaded documents to verify that NOVA retrieved related information and that its answers were grounded in the available content.

## Result

A working platform with the NOVA assistant integrated.

No formal precision metric was defined for the RAG system, so no accuracy percentage is reported. A more mature version should measure retrieval relevance, answer precision and cases with insufficient evidence.

## Learnings

I learned how to integrate generative AI into a real application and how a full RAG flow works, from uploading documents to generating answers.

Today I would work on evaluation from the start, with a set of questions and expected answers to objectively measure retrieval and answer quality.
